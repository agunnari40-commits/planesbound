# Story Apocrypha: The Full Arc

*Morrowroot, codename Black Winter Wheel. A frozen claim of forty acres, a sliding wild, a dark lighthouse, & the light everyone wants.*

**How to read this page.** Every fact carries a tag. **[S1]** to **[S14]** point to a source in `sources.md`. **[FILLER]** marks something invented for this package to close a small gap, drawn from the mythos; the owner can overrule any of it. **[XWALK]** marks a name match between the Planesbound atlas & the Morrowroot lore that was inferred from matching content, not stated by a source. Story text follows the owner's house style: "&" in place of the word it replaces, & no hyphens.

**Source ranking for story.** The Morrowroot Apocrypha [S1] wins, then Finishing the Wheel [S2], then the Production Handoff [S3]. The Planesbound atlas [S8] is the public face of the same world under its older names. The original Design Handoff [S5] is legacy & supplies modes & motifs, not names.

## 1. The Cosmos

Every plane hangs from the **Morrowroot**, a world tree whose roots grow downward through nothing & braid into a lattice. Each realm is a ring of bark on that lattice; each leyline is a vein of its sap. Together the rings form the **Rimwheel**: an outer band of sixteen moral realms, an inner band of elemental shores, & at its hub the **Axlestone**, a pillar of neutral ground that once sat dead center. [S1]

Sap rises from the taproot to feed the **Hearthsun**, the sun every realm shares. When sap flows, the Hearthsun burns gold. When it is pinched, light curdles into the **Gloamwound**, a black sun hanging opposite. The two pulse in step because they drink from the same root. [S1] On the Planesbound atlas these are the Daystar (The Sun) & the Midnight Sun, "a hole the shape of day, growing in pulses that match the true Sun's dimming." [S8] [XWALK]

The owner named the tree story's planting ground: the new tree goes into the hunt realm (the Beastlands on the atlas, Antlerwild in the game). [S11]

## 4. BEYOND

Four places sit outside the Rimwheel & obey none of its directions. [S1] The atlas groups its outside places as sites around the rim & names the far worlds section **BEYOND**. [S13]

* **The Askew.** A realm of wrong angles where corners point inward. Its creatures are footnotes to geometry; it leaks wherever a seam is soldered badly. [S1] Atlas: Far Realms, "a warning mark, not a road." [S8] [XWALK]
* **The Mistlock.** A closed domain of fog, where each prisoner rules a single grieving village & cannot leave. Its mists gather what the Pitchfrost loses. [S1] Atlas: Domain of Dread. [XWALK]
* **The Undercroft Registry.** A basement institution that catalogs & contains dangerous things in numbered cells. It is polite & very interested in Ani. [S1] Atlas: Miskatonic, "Basement & SCP Program." [XWALK]
* **The Starfold Prism.** A heavenly geometry of facets, each facet an oath given shape. Two matter: the **Rimevow Facet** (the Frozen Oath, "nothing shall sunder again") & the **Emberbond Facet** (the Burning Pact, "whatever is broken shall be forged anew"). The Rimeward was drawn with geometry borrowed from this prism. [S1] Atlas: Lumia, "Frozen Oath & Burning Pact... Do not swear both vows in the same breath." [S8] [XWALK]
* **The Far Worlds.** Other games bleeding in, reachable only through the Askew: Calvenreach (a refuge of sealed cathedrals under siege), Scaurland (scrap & sand caravans), Pebblefold (cozy creature raising), the Shardmarch (floating continents round a dead core), & Ossuarch (an undead empire of bone crowned legions, the Gravesworn's distant kin). All are out of v1. [S1] [S2] On the atlas this is BEYOND, "a viewing room," tuned with a Compass Orb & a wizard's HUD. [S8]

## 5. Hidden History: the Five Breaks

Told in order. [S2]

1. **Before the Rimwheel.** The Morrowroot grows downward through nothing, a single root with no rings.
2. **The Bough Parting, First Sundering.** The roots braid into a lattice; the rings form; the Axlestone takes the center.
3. **The Kindling.** The Hearthsun is lit from rising sap; Leylit wake as couriers; the Kindled Moot gathers.
4. **The Seam Rending, Second Sundering.** The shores separate & the Seams open; Rimeseam breathes the first true cold.
5. **The Founding.** Root keepers raise **Duskmarrow** at the fork beneath Antlerwild, in the tree's shade.
6. **The Crack.** The lattice strains; the ruler of Duskmarrow cuts a taproot to save the city & swears a half oath.
7. **The Fall.** The city falls into shadow anyway; the Morrowseed drops into the Sifting with the sawdust.
8. **The First Lamp.** The Regent builds the Magpie Light on Bearing Head & the Needlesphere to find the cut root, burning the Ember Elder's years as fuel.
9. **The Bell Splitting, Third Sundering.** The bark tears around the wound; the Axlestone slips; Geodehall is locked under Underheart.
10. **The Vanishing.** The Regent loses the Needlesphere in the Sifting & disappears; the Moot forbids the lamp. Ledgerfast's clerks count the Shear fourth because they found it last.
11. **The Long Winter.** The Ten Towns raise the Rimeward; the Pitchfrost wakes; the Gravesworn dies in its first winter; the first settler seals the Tithe Chest.
12. **The Present.** Nightburrs root, the slide begins, Ani takes the Lantern Seat, & the Hunter claims forty acres.

The secret break is **the Quiet Shear**: the root cut beneath Duskmarrow. The public break is **the Bell Splitting**. [S1] The atlas numbers these differently (see the open questions file, Q4).

## 6. The One Story

The winter, the sliding wild, the ice ring, & the lighthouse are four faces of the same wound. [S1]

1. **The slide.** The cut taproot lies under Antlerwild's eastern reach. As the scar reopens, that edge loses its anchor & drifts toward the Churn. Herds vanish into fog. A living cutting of the Morrowroot, set in the wound, is the only anchor strong enough to hold the realm. [S1] Atlas: "The Beastlands is falling into Limbo." [S8]
2. **The Rimeward.** Generations ago the Ten Towns raised a ring of ice across the dale's east: river water poured in layered courses, clockwork cold bartered from the Cogwold, & desperate geometry copied from the Starfold Prism. It holds the slide, but only by freezing everything near it. [S1] Atlas: The Ice Wall, "river water, Mechanus cold, & desperate geometry." [S8]
3. **The Pitchfrost (the Black Winter).** Ice drawn with the Rimevow's geometry carried the Frozen Oath with it. The oath woke as a living winter whose only aim is that nothing sunder again, even if nothing ever moves again. [S1] The original handoff called it the Black Winter, a living antagonist that possesses creatures & tries to claim the homestead. [S5]
4. **The Magpie Light.** A black & white tower on **Bearing Head**, the Rimeward's eastern headland, keeps the last true bearing: the direction of the cut taproot. Its lamp burns Leylit light. Each night lit costs the burning Leylit a year of memory, which is why it went dark. [S1] Atlas: The Lighthouse, "The Last True Bearing," seven flights, a cistern, & a lantern room facing the chaos wound. [S8]
5. **The seeds.** **Nightburrs** are dark seeds sown by the Hollow Regent. They root in leyline seams & solder planes shut, hoping to graft Duskmarrow back onto the root. Each soldered seam pinches the sap, dims the Hearthsun, & swells the Gloamwound. The Pitchfrost welcomes them, since sealed planes cannot sunder. [S1] Atlas: "Dark seeds try to solder the planes shut." [S8]

**Ani's place.** Geodehall's crystals store more light than any realm. Whoever leads it holds enough light to relight the Magpie Light without destroying a single Leylit, or enough to power a final soldering. The Regent wants it to finish the seal, the Pitchfrost wants it frozen in place, & the Old Beast wants it kept underground. [S1]

**The Old Beast & the cost of the lock.** Underheart's ancient grazer of stone accepted Geodehall beneath itself to hide Mineral's light from the Regent. The price falls on Mineral: no sky, fading crystals, dimmer Leylit births, & a tithe of light every season. Ani resents the cage; the Beast resents being called a jailer. Their quarrel is the mid campaign hinge. [S1]

**The Sifting's role.** Dust is the realm that tells the truth. Every expedition turns up shards of the Shear, & it holds the **Morrowseed**, a cutting that fell in the Quiet Shear. [S1]

## 8. Powers & Their Ends

[S2] unless tagged.

* **The Hollow Regent.** Wants to graft Duskmarrow back onto the root. Secret: on the night of the cut it swore "nothing shall sunder again," meaning to add that the cut would be mended, but the city fell first. That half oath became the Rimevow. Twist: the Regent & the Pitchfrost serve the same broken sentence from opposite ends.
* **The Old Beast.** Hides Mineral's light. Secret: the tithe feeds its failing eyes. It fears being needed by no one more than it fears a free Geodehall.
* **The Kindled Moot.** Keeps the Leylit from burning. Secret: the Ember Elder was the first lamp's fuel & no longer remembers why it forbade the lamp. The forbidding was grief, not law.
* **The Pitchfrost.** Keeps its oath. Secret: it cannot hear an answer from anyone who has ever broken a vow. That is why only the Youngest, a child who has sworn nothing, can speak the counter oath.
* **The Churnglint.** Watches the crack. Secret: the Churn is frightened; it cannot digest a living realm. Every riddle points toward a Nightburr.
* **The Undercroft Registry.** Catalogs danger. Secret: it holds Cell Zero, the shattered first lens of the Magpie Light. It fears Ani as a key, not a weapon.

## 9. The Black Winter Clock

The Pitchfrost escalates in five stages, one step at each season's close. A **hold** cancels the next advance; uprooting a Nightburr or relighting the Magpie Light earns one, & only one can be banked. A Nightburr that roots spends the banked hold or pushes the clock at once. [S2] [S3]

| Stage | Name | Effect [S1] |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | (unmarked) | A raw claim with 10 Warmth & one wall |
| 1 | Hoarstep | Frost at dawn; hunting yields drop by one |
| 2 | Glasswater | Rivers freeze; trade with the Ten Towns closes |
| 3 | Long Hush | Sound dies outdoors; possession becomes possible |
| 4 | Stillmarrow | Creatures & people freeze where they stand; raids double |
| 5 | Last Bell | The dale stops. Game over. |

**Possession.** From Long Hush on, a character who ends a turn outside warmth with 3 or more Chill is possessed for one round. A Warmth card or the Mother Aspect's breath breaks it early. Three possessions in one session make a frost thrall, cleansed only at the lit Magpie Light. [S1]

**The Pitchfrost cannot be killed. It is answered.** [S1]

## 11. Endings

Four endings, chosen by campaign state. Triggers are the production defaults in the play modes file. [S2] [S3]

* **Main.** The bargain holds, the lamp burns, the seed takes root, the oath is spoken. The forty acres become the **Eleventh Hearth**. Ani holds the Lantern Seat under open sky. The Gravesworn keeps the Magpie Light. Duskmarrow rises as the seventeenth Hinge Town.
* **Mercy.** Every power is spared the price it fears. The homestead stays a homestead, warmed by the Churnglint as a weathervane. The Old Beast sees again.
* **Hard Cost.** Geodehall's store ran dry, so the lamp burns Leylit light after all. Ani forgets the household's names; the Gravesworn becomes Ani's memory.
* **Bittersweet.** The Ember Elder burns one last night to remember, & fades. Ani joins the Moot as the new Ember Elder. The Gravesworn walks into the Askew to answer Ossuarch.

## 12. WW4: T H E SINGULARITY

**Owner canon, declared 10 November 2026.** The text below is the owner's own, copied uncut from [S6]. Do not edit it. Source post: x.com/GlowingSporeBat/status/2120296589533028607. Hero image: the singularity vortex image in the assets lore folder (a blue white vortex of light & ice shards in a moonlit snowy pine forest).

> Intelligence has already stopped waiting for permission. Models train the next generation while the last is still learning; robot arms print their own successors & never pause for a purchase order. The cost of thought is falling toward the cost of electricity, & electricity itself is being driven down by machines that never sleep or negotiate.
>
> In the space that opens in the future, the real work begins. When competence is no longer scarce, the machines can optimize everything except the one thing that still has to be chosen: which local weirdnesses are worth keeping. Which friendships. Which rituals. Which stubborn, inefficient, beautiful things we all refuse to let be solved away.
>
> We will protect those by hand, deliberately, & the way one protects a small light in a rising wind. Not because the air is hostile, but bc the light is ours & the machines were never asked to care. We will build until the floor rises under everyone who wants to stand on it, & we will leave the ceiling exactly where the old world left it while making zero excuses.
>
> Winter is possessed, there's a Wyrm in you eating you alive, & we will Win if we follow The Way.

**How it threads into the game. [FILLER]** These are suggestions, not canon, for the owner to accept or strike.

* *"Winter is possessed."* The Pitchfrost is an oath wearing weather; it possesses those who stand outside warmth too long. The game already says this in mechanics (possession from Long Hush on).
* *"A Wyrm in you eating you alive."* Read inward: Chill. Cold gathers on a body one point at a time until the body is no longer its own. A frost thrall is a person the Wyrm finished. Optional flavor line on the frost thrall card.
* *"A small light in a rising wind."* The Magpie Light, the Warding Lantern, & Ani. The whole win path is protecting one small light by hand.
* *"Which local weirdnesses are worth keeping."* The Ten Towns, each with its odd trade & rumor, & the household's beats. The Boasts track (Warmest Hand, Longest Watch) is the game's quiet ledger of what the party chose to keep.
* *"The Way."* The true bearing that the lamp keeps. The Needlesphere points The Way.
* **Placement.** A WW4 codex page unlocked after the Last Muster in every ending, shown full bleed over the vortex art, owner text uncut. [FILLER]

Note: a shortened gazetteer draft of this canon exists [S7], but it trims & rewords the owner's text & was flagged as not ready. This package uses only the uncut canon.

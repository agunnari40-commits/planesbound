## 1. The Claim: Forty Acres

**Sourced facts**
- The Hunter claims forty acres in **Rimmerdale**, the frozen northern reach of Antlerwild. [S1] (Legacy wording: "40-acre claimed land in Ten Towns under the Black Winter" [S5].)
- On the claim (legacy list): the cabin, the fire pit, the foundation for a new barn, the sled construction area. [S5]
- The **Tithe Chest** lies under the homestead floor, forged & blood sealed by the first settler to bind a shard of the Rimevow to the land; it is why the homestead stays livable. The **Churnglint** hovers over it. [S1]
- A **barrow line** runs east of the claim; the Gravesworn kneels there in season 1; bone frost forms on its stones as an Ossuarch bleed. [S2]
- The **Watch Post** is rebuilt facing the Askew seam (season 6). [S2]
- Structures sit on a **12 × 12 grid**. [S3]
- At the end of the campaign the forty acres become the **Eleventh Hearth**. [S1]

**Derived.** 40 acres = 1,742,400 sq ft ≈ a 1,320 ft square, so each of the 144 homestead cells is a 110 ft lot. Raids are fought "on the homestead grid in 5 ft squares" [S3]; both can be true if the raid view is a tactical zoom of the yard. **[FILLER] default:** the raid view is a 32 × 32 grid of 5 ft squares (160 ft) centered on the cabin cell; any structure in the 3 × 3 homestead cells around the cabin is projected into it by relative position; palisade segments on the compound edge become the raid perimeter. See `open-questions.md` Q6.

**[FILLER] default starting layout (12 × 12, x→east, y→south).** East is the danger side (Rimeward, barrows, the slide), so defenses face east.

## 3. The Rimeward, Bearing Head & the Magpie Light

- **The Rimeward**: a made ring of ice across the dale's east: river water in layered courses, clockwork cold bartered from the Cogwold, & geometry copied from the Starfold Prism. Holds the slide by freezing everything near it. [S1] Atlas twin: **the Ice Wall**, a sheer pale lattice cliff, frost runes recut after every surge, barracks & rune shops in its hollows, gates at the four quarters, thickest on the south; "do not climb the outer face during a stutter of the Lighthouse." [S8]
- **Bearing Head**: the Rimeward's eastern headland; a cairn there (the Bearing Stone) is older than the lamp. [S1] [S2]
- **The Magpie Light**: a black & white tower; its lamp burns Leylit light at the cost of a year of memory per night, so it went dark. [S1] Atlas: seven flights, a cistern of river water, a lantern room facing the chaos wound on purpose; the top floor is called **the Bearing**; reached by the headland road from the inner wall gate or by river to the pier. From the Bearing a curtain opens to the Inner Ring & the Will o' Wisp Race. [S8]
- **Beam states 0 to 3** are specified for art (dark, test, lit, beam through the Needlesphere) in the style apocrypha §10. [S4]
- **Board role**: the Lighthouse space on the Dale board; start of the Lighthouse Race; cleansing point for frost thralls once lit. [S1] [FILLER]

**Original motif (legacy):** "Recurring visual & narrative motif. Seam between worlds. Glowing gate symbol (vertical cyan-purple bars) on ice mountains." [S5] The current palette forbids teal-cyan & caps violet, so the gate glyph needs a redesign in palette (Q9).

## 5. The Wheel Board (planes)

Contents required [S3]: the Axlestone hub, the Hinge Towns with Antlerbrook nearest the hub, & one space per cameo realm. Five realms are interactive in v1 (Antlerwild with Rimmerdale, the Churn's eastern edge, Underheart, Geodehall, the Sifting); every other realm is a board space & a cameo event card only. [S1]

**Layout source.** Use the Planesbound campaign map as the Wheel board's visual template: `assets/maps/campaign-map-nolabels.jpeg` (1600 × 900, md5 `3c854e3c69a2ab052d21f7ba7ff2fe2e`) with plane card centers & sizes in percent from the live site (`reference/planesbound-folios.json`, fields `x,y,w,h`) & the Inner orb centers from `assets/maps/inner-orb-coords-wheel-view.csv`. [S8] [S10] The atlas Inner view uses `assets/maps/inner-map.jpeg` with the first 14 entries of the folios JSON as hotspots. [S8] These images are **layout reference**; final board art is stylized 3D (see `art-style.md`).

**Hub conflict.** The atlas puts the Beastlands *in* the hub where the Outlands should be [S8]; Morrowroot keeps the Axlestone as the hub with Antlerbrook *nearest* it [S1]. **[FILLER] default:** the center space is the Axlestone, drawn as the drifted pillar sitting inside Antlerwild's wild (matching the atlas art), with Antlerbrook as the hub's first ring space. Q5.

**Wheel space table** (game id → atlas id & percent position) is in `data/board_wheel.json`. Hidden space: Duskmarrow, revealed in season 8 [S3].

## 6. Gates & Travel

- **Veilway**: the curtain road of mist between shores; entry costs 1 Lightshard per traveler; shut until the first raid is survived. [S1] [S3]
- **Gate conditions** (sourced examples): Antlerwild "carry a trophy from a big game hunt"; Underheart "pay Ani's season tithe in advance." [S3] Others [FILLER] in `data/realms.json`.
- **Atlas travel rules worth keeping as flavor** [S8]: the curtain road is thrown from the Lighthouse lamp "when the Daystar bearing is clean"; "do not approach during a stutter"; Earth "approach the Beast only with intent stated at the table"; Lumia "do not swear both vows in the same breath."
- **Compass Orb** (atlas) = **Needlesphere** (game) [XWALK]: "a party sat-link back toward the Lighthouse when the Daystar pulse is clean. It is a device for the chase, not a second atlas." [S9]

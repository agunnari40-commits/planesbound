# Finishing the Wheel

*Eight seasons, five realms, ten towns & one cut root, carried through to the thaw.*

---

## Clock Math

The clock opens every campaign at stage zero, unmarked, & advances one stage at each season's close. A **hold** cancels the next advance. The party earns a hold by uprooting a Nightburr or by relighting the Magpie Light, & may bank only one hold at a time. A Nightburr that roots spends a banked hold first; with none banked, it pushes the clock at once. Uprooting a Nightburr also returns its seam to flowing sap, so the Hearthsun brightens one notch on the campaign panel.

| Season | Stage in play | Hold earned | Stage after close |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | zero | none | Hoarstep |
| 2 | Hoarstep | none | Glasswater |
| 3 | Glasswater | Nightburr, Churn edge | Glasswater |
| 4 | Glasswater | none | Long Hush |
| 5 | Long Hush | Nightburr, Dim Nursery | Long Hush |
| 6 | Long Hush | none | Stillmarrow |
| 7 | Stillmarrow | lamp relit | Stillmarrow |
| 8 | Stillmarrow | finale | thaw or Last Bell |

The full telling spends four advances & three holds, so the finale lands in season eight with Last Bell one close away. A swift party can reach the root fork by season five; a careless one meets Last Bell after five closes.

## Season Arc

### Season One

**Pressure.** No stage yet; the threat is a raw claim with 10 Warmth & one wall. **Beat.** The Hunter claims forty acres in Rimmerdale. Ani arrives at Coldharrow on the first turn, trailed by the Gravesworn, because Geodehall's charts show a light under the homestead. That light is the Tithe Chest, & the Churnglint rises over it the night it is opened. **Realm.** Rimmerdale only; the Veilway stays shut until the first raid is survived. **Raid.** Six frost raiders walk out of the Rimeward's glare, their tracks pointing backward.

* **Eldest Child:** lights the forge's first coal & burns a thumb learning the bellows.
* **Middle Child:** counts the cellar, finds boards that ring hollow, & uncovers the Tithe Chest.
* **Youngest:** hears the chest hum in a key the Fizzlet answers.
* **Hearth Father:** sets the first palisade & vows never to leave the cabin while raiders stand in the snow.
* **Snow Crowned Tracker:** reads the backward tracks & learns the raiders are poured, not born.
* **Mother Aspect:** her breath thaws the chest's rime, which hisses at her like a kettle.
* **Gravesworn:** kneels at the barrow line east of the claim, ember full at three.
* **Ani:** pays the first light tithe to Underheart at dawn & admits the Lantern Seat makes Ani hunted.

### Season Two

**Pressure.** Hoarstep frost cuts every hunt by one Food. **Beat.** Herds walk east into fog that should not move. The Tracker follows them through the Veilway to Antlerbrook, where the hunt realm sits nearest the hub. **Realm.** Antlerwild. **Raid.** Slide beasts, herd animals unmoored by the drift, stampede the palisade in panic rather than malice.

* **Eldest Child:** forges arrowheads from slide beast antler that hold heat.
* **Middle Child:** builds the Smokehouse ledger & refuses to let a scrap spoil.
* **Youngest:** tells the Fizzlet the ice is "saying a word with no ending."
* **Hearth Father:** teaches the Eldest to bank a fire overnight.
* **Snow Crowned Tracker:** finds the Fork Hollow, where snow sinks toward a root no map shows.
* **Mother Aspect:** calms a stampeding herd mother with breath instead of fire.
* **Gravesworn:** recognizes the Hollow & goes silent for a whole turn.
* **Ani:** sees the slide is a wound, not weather, & asks the Gravesworn what it remembers.

### Season Three

**Pressure.** Glasswater freezes the rivers; trade with the Ten Hearths closes, & stores must carry the household. **Beat.** The Churnglint trades a riddle for Warmth: "the door that sews itself shut stands where the herd forgot the ground." Its answer is the first Nightburr, soldering the seam on the Churn's eastern edge. Uprooting it earns the first hold. **Realm.** The Churn's eastern edge. **Raid.** Nightburr thralls, hunting the Warding Lantern.

* **Eldest Child:** melts a thrall's burr husk in the forge & learns it burns violet.
* **Middle Child:** rations by the Smokehouse ledger & keeps the household fed through the closed roads.
* **Youngest:** hears the uprooted burr "sigh like a door that wanted to stay open."
* **Hearth Father:** rebuilds two palisade segments under thrall fire & keeps the shutters closed.
* **Snow Crowned Tracker:** reads the Churn's shifting ground by the snow that refuses to fall on it.
* **Mother Aspect:** shields a Driftmeadow herd from dissolving into thought.
* **Gravesworn:** loses its first ember to the season's frost & stands guard over Ani regardless.
* **Ani:** holds the uprooted burr & feels it reaching toward somewhere below Antlerwild.

### Season Four

**Pressure.** Glasswater holds; the close will bring Long Hush. **Beat.** The mid campaign hinge. Ani pays the tithe at Underheart's gate, then refuses the next one in the Grazing Deep & demands the lock be lifted. The Old Beast answers that it hides Mineral's light from the master of a fallen city. The ceiling cracks under the quarrel, & cold violet smoke rises through it. **Realm.** Underheart. **Raid.** The Crack Swarm: thralls climbing out of the ceiling breach beneath Coldharrow, led by a shadow lamplighter carrying an unlit wick.

* **Eldest Child:** asks the Hearth Father what a lighthouse lens is made of.
* **Middle Child:** sends the season's surplus to Coldharrow by sled before the hush.
* **Youngest:** hears the Beast's voice through the hearthstones, sad rather than angry.
* **Hearth Father:** admits his grandsire helped pour the Rimeward's last course.
* **Snow Crowned Tracker:** reads frost script on the cracked ceiling: a city's name written in shadow.
* **Mother Aspect:** sits between Ani & the Beast when tempers flare, warm & immovable.
* **Gravesworn:** remembers its own death, poured into the Rimeward with the other barrow hunters.
* **Ani:** says the word "jailer" aloud & regrets it before the echo fades.

### Season Five

**Pressure.** Long Hush: sound dies outdoors, horns fail, & possession begins. **Beat.** Ani takes the party home to Geodehall. Leylit in the Dim Nursery are born dimmer each season, & one cradle has gone dark around a Nightburr. Uprooting it earns the second hold. Resting in Geodehall refills the Gravesworn's ember. **Realm.** Geodehall. **Raid.** The Silent Raid, fought without calls; the Fizzlet carries every order through frost.

* **Eldest Child:** cuts a test lens from Geodehall quartz brought home in the Tracker's pack.
* **Middle Child:** packs supplies in labeled bundles for an expedition to come.
* **Youngest:** hears a voice in the ice say "nothing" & stop there.
* **Hearth Father:** breaks a possession on the Tracker by sharing his last Warmth card.
* **Snow Crowned Tracker:** is possessed once & carries the winter's hush in her voice for a day.
* **Mother Aspect:** breathes on three possessed allies in one raid & earns the Warmest Hand Boast.
* **Gravesworn:** refills to three embers & says the barrow's old hunting oath for Ani.
* **Ani:** sees the crystals fading under sunless rock & resolves to bring Geodehall into the sky.

### Season Six

**Pressure.** Long Hush deepens; Chill gathers fast. **Beat.** The dust remembers. The Needlesphere lies half buried in the Sawdust Banks, & two shards surface beside it: the Cut & the First Lamp. The Undercroft Registry sends a polite envoy offering the shattered first lens for a catalog entry on Ani. **Realm.** The Sifting. **Raid.** The Askew Leak: wrong angle beasts slip through a soldered seam near Owlgate, their corners pointing inward.

* **Eldest Child:** sees the test lens split the light into the wrong colors & starts over.
* **Middle Child:** trades preserved meat to Owlgate by sled despite the frozen roads.
* **Youngest:** hears the ice say "nothing shall sunder" & wait for more.
* **Hearth Father:** rebuilds the Watch Post facing the Askew seam.
* **Snow Crowned Tracker:** follows grain patterns in the dust back to the night of the cut.
* **Mother Aspect:** lifts the Needlesphere from the dust with her teeth.
* **Gravesworn:** loses an ember at the close & fights one raid round as a foe.
* **Ani:** declines the Registry's offer but keeps its card.

### Season Seven

**Pressure.** Stillmarrow freezes people where they stand & doubles every raid. **Beat.** Deep in the Sifting the last shard waits: the Hollow Regent's own oath, sworn the night of the cut, with the Morrowseed beneath it. Ani faces the Kindled Moot & the Old Beast at once & strikes the bargain. At Bearing Head the Eldest Child's lens is set, & Ani lights the lamp from Geodehall's stored light. The lit lamp earns the final hold. **Realm.** The Sifting deep, then Underheart. **Raid.** The Frozen Muster, doubled: frost raiders, thralls, & frost thralls marching under a single rime banner.

* **Eldest Child:** forges the true lens from quartz, Lightshard, & a sliver of the first lens.
* **Middle Child:** fills the sleds with the final expedition's stores.
* **Youngest:** hears the full half oath & asks why the ice stops talking in the middle.
* **Hearth Father:** keeps the shutters closed through a doubled raid & never once looks away from the door.
* **Snow Crowned Tracker:** is possessed three times in the doubled raid, turns frost thrall, & is the first soul cleansed at the lit lamp.
* **Mother Aspect:** carries the lens to Bearing Head on her back through Stillmarrow cold.
* **Gravesworn:** stands lamp watch the first night the beam turns.
* **Ani:** spends Mineral's stored light so no Leylit pays a year of memory.

### Season Eight

**Pressure.** Stillmarrow holds; the close rings Last Bell unless the story ends first. **Beat.** The beam passes through the Needlesphere & points east through the slide to the cut taproot beneath Duskmarrow. The Regent meets the party at the root fork. The Hunter offers the bearing, & together they plant the Morrowseed. After the planting the party rides the beam home, because the Pitchfrost musters its last raid on the homestead; when the hearth holds, the Youngest speaks the counter oath. **Realm.** Antlerwild, at the root fork. **Raid.** The Last Muster, a doubled wave that breaks the moment the oath is spoken.

* **Eldest Child:** watches the Bearing Head beam from the cabin window & knows the lens holds.
* **Middle Child:** opens the last cask for the household & sets aside a share for every Hearth.
* **Youngest:** speaks the counter oath to the Rimevow shard inside the Tithe Chest, safe at the hearth while the Fizzlet carries each word through frost.
* **Hearth Father:** holds the door through the Last Muster's first round until the party lands on the beam.
* **Snow Crowned Tracker:** reads the slide's fog like script & finds the path to the fork.
* **Mother Aspect:** breathes on the Morrowseed to wake it in frozen soil.
* **Gravesworn:** carries Ani's light into the fork where shadow is thickest.
* **Ani:** meets the Regent eye to eye, light to hollow.

## Realm Folios

### Antlerwild with Rimmerdale

**Overview.** Pine resin, hoofbeat, & a wind that always smells of the next valley. Rimmerdale's frozen north gives way to herd roads running into the eastern fog. **Sites.** The Shedding Yards, a plain carpeted with dropped antler that chimes underfoot; Hornsong Crossing, where herd roads braid & echoes run a second ahead of sound; the Fork Hollow, a bowl of sinking snow above the root fork. **Inhabitants.** The Herd Warden, who counts every animal lost to the fog; the Velvet Keeper, who reads antlers for omens; the Bone Ferrier, who crosses the slide by sled for a fee. **Objective.** Mark the Fork Hollow & carry a trophy home. **Hazard.** Slide fog: end a turn in it & drift one hex east. **Shard.** The Fall: Duskmarrow sinking into shadow while its bells still ring.

### The Churn, Eastern Edge

**Overview.** Colors without names, ground that reconsiders itself, & a taste of copper whenever someone thinks too loudly. Here the thought soup laps at Antlerwild's drifting meadows. **Sites.** The Fraying Shelf, where solid land unravels into thread; the Thoughtpools, wells that show what a traveler fears next; Driftmeadow, a torn piece of Antlerwild still grazed by bewildered herds. **Inhabitants.** The Unfinished Cartographer, who draws maps that become the land; the Shapeless Shepherd, who keeps lost herds in imagined fences; the Seam Mender, who stitches torn ground with string. **Objective.** Uproot the Nightburr soldering the seam. **Hazard.** Thought made matter: each failed Wisdom save adds 1 Chill shaped as a cold memory. **Shard.** The Drift: the night the cut root's sap first ran east, & the Churn learned to hunger.

### Underheart

**Overview.** Endless stone, the breath of slow lungs, & the deep chewing of something huge grazing on bedrock. Light here is a rumor. **Sites.** The Grazing Deep, where the Old Beast feeds; the Chewstone Galleries, tunnels carved by its teeth; the Ceiling Seal, a vault of fused stone pressed over Geodehall. **Inhabitants.** The Seal Mason, who patches cracks with prayer & mortar; the Stone Choir Mistress, whose hum steadies the ceiling; the Tunnel Midwife, who births stone calves in the dark. **Objective.** Reach the Grazing Deep & hear the Beast out. **Hazard.** Ceiling shift: every turn roll 1d6; on a 1 the hex collapses. **Shard.** The Bell: the treaty night, & the Beast's choice to take Mineral beneath itself.

### Geodehall

**Overview.** Crystal halls ringing faintly, light stacked in walls like grain in a silo, & a stale warmth fading for want of sky. Ani's seat. **Sites.** The Lantern Seat, a throne of quartz where Mineral's light can be spent; the Charge Wells, shafts where crystals drink light; the Dim Nursery, cradles where Leylit are born. **Inhabitants.** The Facet Steward, who counts the stored light; the Nursery Keeper, who sings to dim newborns; the Quartz Chancellor, who wants Ani to sell light to the Registry. **Objective.** Uproot the Nightburr in the Dim Nursery & rest Ani at the Seat. **Hazard.** Fading charge: Lightshard costs rise by one each turn spent here. **Shard.** The Hidden Light: Mineral's founders agreeing to vanish rather than feed the Regent's search.

### The Sifting

**Overview.** Dust in every breath, soft dunes of everything ground small, & whispers that rise when a traveler stands still. Dust is the realm that tells the truth. **Sites.** The Sawdust Banks, where the Shear's shavings came to rest; the Hourglass Steppe, where grains fall upward on the hour; the Settling Pool, a basin of motionless dust where the Morrowseed waits. **Inhabitants.** The Grain Reader, who divines events from dust patterns; the Sweeper, who tends the banks & resents visitors; the Dust Widow, who repeats the last word spoken by the dead. **Objective.** Recover the Needlesphere, then the Morrowseed. **Hazard.** Memory drift: each turn a traveler forgets one card in hand. **Shard.** The Oath, the last shard, with the Cut & the First Lamp surfacing beside the Needlesphere.

## The Ten Hearths

**Kettlecross.** The market hearth, trading cured hide & copper pots. Rumor holds that its bell tower was once a Hinge Town bell, thrown out of the wheel. In the finale its square hosts the thaw feast that names the Eleventh Hearth.

**Saltwick.** Salt from frozen springs keeps the dale's meat sweet. Rumor says its spring water tastes of tears when the Pitchfrost is near. In the finale its salt seals the Morrowseed's cradle against rot.

**Lowbraid.** Rope & sled harness, braided from herd hair. Its weavers claim one cord was spun from the Morrowroot's own fiber. In the finale its rope lowers the party into the root fork.

**Tallowmoor.** Candles & lamp oil from bog fat. Rumor whispers the town keeps a flame lit since the Tallow Spires gave it as a gift. In the finale its oil soaks the wick that carries Bearing Head's beam into the slide.

**Fenspire.** Marsh lights bottled for travelers, though no one agrees whether they lead astray or home. Rumor says a Leylit lives among the bog lanterns, hiding from the Moot. In the finale the hiding Leylit testifies before the Moot for Ani.

**Drumrest.** Drums & signal horns, useless after Long Hush. Rumor: its drummers can still beat a rhythm the Pitchfrost cannot silence. In the finale that rhythm calls the Ten Hearths together for the thaw.

**Haskeep.** Iron & nails from a mine dug too deep. Its miners swear they heard grazing in the rock. In the finale Haskeep iron rings the Needlesphere's bead to steady its swing.

**Owlgate.** Scouts & watchers, with owls trained to carry notes. Rumor says the owls avoid one corner of town where the angles feel wrong. In the finale Owlgate's watch closes the Askew seam with Mother Aspect breath on the cracked angle.

**Brindle Ford.** Cattle & cheese from the dale's only river crossing. Rumor holds that the ford was where the Rimeward's first water course was poured. In the finale its river runs again as the Rimeward melts back into it.

**Coldharrow.** The town nearest the Rimeward, trading ice blocks & cold lore. Rumor says its oldest families can still hear the Rimeward singing. In the finale it becomes the dale's gate to Antlerwild's new east.

## Powers & Their Ends

### The Hollow Regent

**Motive.** To graft Duskmarrow back onto the root & undo the Shear. **Secret.** The Regent swore the night of the cut that nothing would sunder again, meaning to add that the cut would be mended, but the city fell before the second half was spoken. That half oath became the Rimevow. **Twist.** The Regent & the Pitchfrost serve the same broken sentence from opposite ends: one seals every plane to stop the sundering, the other freezes everything to stop the motion. **Ends.** In the main ending the Regent becomes gatekeeper of the seventeenth Hinge Town. In the hard cost ending the Regent steps into the wound as the root's living keeper, hollow at last filled.

### The Old Beast

**Motive.** To keep Mineral's light hidden from the Regent's search. **Secret.** The tithe does not seal the ceiling; it feeds the Beast's failing eyes, which have watched for the Regent so long in the dark they are going blind. **Twist.** The Beast fears freedom for Geodehall less than it fears being needed by no one. **Ends.** In the main ending the Beast lends its weight to anchor Antlerwild & then rests. In the mercy ending it rises into Geodehall's light & regains its sight, lighter than stone.

### The Kindled Moot

**Motive.** The Ember Elder keeps memory, the Glint Elder travel, the Ash Elder endings; together they keep the Leylit from burning. **Secret.** The Ember Elder was the first lamp's fuel, & burned so many years in the Regent's service that it no longer remembers why it forbade the lamp. **Twist.** The Moot's forbidding was grief, not law. **Ends.** In the main ending the Moot blesses the lamp lit from Mineral's light. In the bittersweet ending the Ember Elder chooses to recover its lost years by burning one night more, & fades.

### The Pitchfrost

**Motive.** To keep its oath. **Secret.** It cannot hear an answer from anyone who has ever broken a vow. **Twist.** That is why only the Youngest can speak the counter oath: a child who has never sworn anything. **Ends.** In the main ending it thaws into an honest winter. In the hard cost ending it survives as a smaller winter that keeps Coldharrow as its own, accepted but never fully answered.

### The Churnglint

**Motive.** To trade riddles for warmth & watch the crack. **Secret.** The Churn itself is frightened; it cannot digest a living realm, & the slide is choking it. **Twist.** Every riddle was a direction toward the Nightburrs, because the Churn needs the slide stopped as badly as the dale does. **Ends.** In the main ending it bows & returns to the Churn. In the mercy ending it stays as the Eleventh Hearth's weathervane, spinning to every true bearing.

### The Undercroft Registry

**Motive.** To catalog every dangerous thing, & nothing is more dangerous than the light Ani commands. **Secret.** The Registry holds Cell Zero, the shattered first lens of the Magpie Light, taken on the night the Regent vanished. **Twist.** The Registry fears Ani not as a weapon but as a key; Mineral's light could open every cell at once. **Ends.** In the main ending it accepts Ani's catalog of light, not of Ani, & files the Gloamwound's cinder in Cell Zero. In the bittersweet ending it keeps Ani under polite watch forever, a single numbered entry.

## The Quiet Shear

1. **Before the Rimwheel.** The Morrowroot grows downward through nothing, a single root with no rings.
2. **The Bough Parting, First Sundering.** The roots braid into a lattice; the rings form; the Axlestone takes the center.
3. **The Kindling.** The Hearthsun is lit from rising sap; Leylit wake as couriers; the Kindled Moot gathers.
4. **The Seam Rending, Second Sundering.** The shores separate & the Seams open; Rimeseam breathes the first true cold.
5. **The Founding.** Root keepers raise Duskmarrow at the fork beneath Antlerwild, in the tree's shade.
6. **The Crack.** The lattice strains; the ruler of Duskmarrow cuts a taproot to save the city & swears the half oath.
7. **The Fall.** The city falls into shadow anyway; the Morrowseed drops into the Sifting with the sawdust.
8. **The First Lamp.** The Regent builds the Magpie Light on Bearing Head & the Needlesphere to find the cut root, burning the Ember Elder's years as fuel.
9. **The Bell Splitting, Third Sundering.** The bark tears around the wound; the Axlestone slips; Geodehall is locked under Underheart.
10. **The Vanishing.** The Regent loses the Needlesphere in the Sifting & disappears; the Moot forbids the lamp. Ledgerfast's clerks later count the Shear fourth because they found it last.
11. **The Long Winter.** The Ten Hearths raise the Rimeward; the Pitchfrost wakes; the Gravesworn dies in its first winter; the first settler seals the Tithe Chest.
12. **The Present.** Nightburrs root, the slide begins, Ani takes the Lantern Seat, & the Hunter claims forty acres.

## Far World Arcs

### Calvenreach

**Premise.** A refuge world of sealed cathedrals, each holding a congregation that has not opened its doors in centuries. The siege outside has no face, only pressure against the walls, a weight that leans harder whenever a candle gutters. Inside, the Vigil Choir keeps the candles lit with hymns timed to the heartbeat of the oldest nave, & the candles are failing one transept at a time. An expansion would play as a siege defense mirror of the homestead: hold a nave instead of a cabin, ration flame instead of Warmth. **Bleed.** Stained glass appears in frost on cabin windows, panes that show a burning city beyond, & the Hearth Father hears hymns when he banks the fire. **Want.** The Abbess Warden asks Ani to come through & refill every candle with Mineral's light. The price is never leaving, & the Warden believes that price is a kindness.

### Scaurland

**Premise.** A wasteland of scrap & sand, where caravans drag rusted cities on treads across dunes in search of water that moves as the sand moves. Its people trade in salvage, keep distance as law, & settle disputes by who can walk farthest without drinking. An expansion would play as a moving homestead: the forty acres become a crawling caravan, & hunting becomes salvage. **Bleed.** Rust creeps across Haskeep iron overnight, sand turns up in sealed Saltwick casks, & the Bone Ferrier swears a rusted tread track crossed the slide. **Want.** The Caravan Mother wants Ani as a compass light, because her people have forgotten the way to the last well. She offers the Eldest Child a salvage forge in trade, a gift the household cannot easily refuse.

### Pebblefold

**Premise.** A cozy world of burrow fairs, where small creatures are raised, groomed, traded, & loved, & where the worst crime is a broken promise to a pet. Its warmth is real, but its creatures are vanishing one by one through a seam that opens toward the Askew. An expansion would play as the softest mode in the game: raise, trade, & rescue, with no raids at all. **Bleed.** Small footprints appear in fresh snow around the Smokehouse, & the Fizzlet cosmetic that nods to Pebblefold begins to remember a home it never had. **Want.** The Fair Keeper wants Ani to find the missing creatures & close the seam, & offers a creature of the household's choice in return. The Youngest wants that creature very badly.

### The Shardmarch

**Premise.** A broken outland of floating continents orbiting a dead core, linked by chains & bridges older than memory. The core once burned like the Hearthsun & went dark when its own root was severed, a mirror of the Quiet Shear on a world with no lamp to find it. An expansion would play as island hopping exploration, each continent a folio, each chain a gamble. **Bleed.** Stones fall upward on Bearing Head, & bridges glimpsed in aurora connect the Ten Hearths for a single night. **Want.** The Chain Steward wants the Morrowseed's sibling, a second cutting rumored to lie in the Sifting, to plant at its dead core. It offers the secret of anchoring land that floats, which Antlerwild could use.

### Ossuarch

**Premise.** An undead empire of bone crowned legions marching under a sunless sky, the Gravesworn's distant kin. Its throne has been empty since its last commander walked into a barrow & never came out, & its legions wait in perfect ranks for a will to follow. An expansion would play as command: the Gravesworn becomes playable, & raids become marches. **Bleed.** Bone frost forms on barrow stones east of the claim, & old war horns sound under the Long Hush when no other sound can. **Want.** The Marrow Herald wants the Gravesworn to come home & take the empty throne, & wants Ani's ember out of its chest so the commander arrives cold & whole. In return the Herald offers the memory of the Gravesworn's living years, which no one else can give back.

## Endings

### The Main Ending

The bargain holds; the lamp burns; the Morrowseed takes root at the fork; the counter oath is spoken. **Household:** the forty acres become the Eleventh Hearth, the Eldest its smith, the Middle Child its steward, the Youngest its oath keeper. **Ani:** holds the Lantern Seat under open sky. **Gravesworn:** keeps the Magpie Light, its ember its own. **Epilogue.** The first spring in an age runs down Rimmerdale's rivers. The Hunter walks his forty acres with the household at his side, & far east a young tree stands where the fog once drifted. On Bearing Head the lamp turns, black & white, & points to a root that is no longer cut.

### The Mercy Ending

The party spares every power the price it fears: the Beast stays beneath, choosing; the Churnglint stays in the hearth; the Regent is forgiven before the planting. **Household:** the homestead stays a homestead, not a town, warmed by the Churnglint's weathervane. **Ani:** keeps Geodehall in Underheart by choice, with skylights cut through the ceiling. **Gravesworn:** returns to its barrow & sleeps, its ember given back to Ani. **Epilogue.** Rimmerdale's winters soften but remain. The Old Beast sees again through Geodehall's light, & Ani visits it each season with no tithe owed.

### The Hard Cost Ending

The lamp is lit from Leylit light after all, because Geodehall's store ran dry. **Household:** survives, but Coldharrow is given to the last winter, its people resettled on the forty acres. **Ani:** pays the memory, forgetting the Lantern Seat & the household's names. **Gravesworn:** becomes Ani's memory, telling the story each night at the lamp. **Epilogue.** The Pitchfrost is smaller but not gone. The Regent stands inside the root as its keeper, & the Hunter carries a stranger's light up Bearing Head every evening, telling it a story it once lived.

### The Bittersweet Ending

The root is mended, but the Ember Elder burns one last night to remember why the lamp was forbidden, & fades. **Household:** the Eleventh Hearth thrives, its children grown into the dale's keepers. **Ani:** joins the Moot as its new Ember Elder, keeper of memory, & leaves the Lantern Seat to the Facet Steward. **Gravesworn:** walks into the Askew to answer Ossuarch's call, & does not return. **Epilogue.** The new tree grows strong. Ani's light hangs over Geodehall's open sky, brighter than before, & the lamp on Bearing Head stands unkept until the Eldest Child climbs it one evening & lights it with a forged lens.

## Lore Codex

1. **Antler Bell.** A bell cast from shed antler, found in the Shedding Yards. It rings only when a herd is lost.
2. **Bearing Stone.** A cairn on Bearing Head older than the lamp. Each stone points to a root.
3. **Bone Ferrier's Toll.** A coin of polished knucklebone. The Ferrier accepts nothing else.
4. **Ceiling Mortar.** A pinch of grey paste from Underheart. It smells of rain that never fell.
5. **Churnglint Riddle.** A riddle scratched on frost. Its answer always points east.
6. **Coldharrow Ice Block.** A cube from the Rimeward's edge. Inside it a river fish still swims slowly.
7. **Dim Cradle.** A crystal cradle from the Dim Nursery. Its glow is half what it should be.
8. **Drumrest Rhythm.** A drumbeat notation. Played under Long Hush, it is still heard.
9. **Dust Widow's Word.** A single word in dust. It is the last thing a stranger said.
10. **Emberbond Ember.** A coal that never cools. It holds the shape of a promise.
11. **Fenspire Lantern.** A bottled marsh light. It swirls toward home or away, depending on the holder.
12. **First Lens Shard.** A sliver of glass from Cell Zero. Light through it bends toward the root fork.
13. **Fork Hollow Snow.** Snow that sinks instead of drifting. It melts toward the ground.
14. **Glint Feather.** A feather of light shed by the Glint Elder. It remembers every road it flew.
15. **Haskeep Nail.** An iron nail from too deep a mine. It hums when held to stone.
16. **Herd Warden's Tally.** A knotted cord counting lost animals. The last knot is still being tied.
17. **Hourglass Grain.** Dust that falls upward. It counts hours backward.

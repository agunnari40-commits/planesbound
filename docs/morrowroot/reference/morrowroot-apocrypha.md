# The Morrowroot Apocrypha
*A frozen homestead, a sliding wild, & the light everyone wants.*

## The Cosmos

Every plane hangs from the **Morrowroot**, a world tree whose roots grow downward through nothing & braid into a lattice. Each realm is a ring of bark on that lattice; each leyline is a vein of its sap. Together the rings form the **Rimwheel**: an outer band of sixteen moral realms, an inner band of elemental shores, & at its hub the **Axlestone**, a pillar of neutral ground that once sat dead center.

Sap rises from the taproot to feed the **Hearthsun**, the sun every realm shares. When sap flows, the Hearthsun burns gold. When it is pinched, light curdles into the **Gloamwound**, a black sun hanging opposite. The two pulse in step because they drink from the same root: every flicker of dimming is light the Gloamwound swallows.

## Outer Ring

Good crowns the top of the band, evil sinks to the bottom, law holds the west, chaos the east. The rest blend between.

Around the Axlestone stand the **Hinge Towns**, one gate town per realm, each half steeped in its neighbor. When the Axlestone drifted, the towns were left in a lopsided ring. Antlerwild's gate town, **Antlerbrook**, now stands nearest the hub, which makes the hunt realm the shortest road to everything.

## The Shores

Between hub & outer band runs the inner ring of elemental shores, joined by the **Veilway**, a curtain road of pale mist that walks beside every plane without touching any.

  **Emberstrand** — fire; glass beaches & burning tides.
  **Tidewell** — water; a sea with no floor.
  **Galeveil** — air; sky without ground.
  **Underheart** — earth; endless stone, home of the Old Beast.
  **Kindling Shore** — raw life light; too much heals until it burns.
  **Ebbing Dark** — raw death chill; it unmakes slowly.
  **The Seams** — four para elemental borders where shores meet: Smokeseam (fire air), Magmaseam (fire earth), Brineseam (water earth, ooze), & **Rimeseam** (air water, ice), the source of every true cold.
  **Geodehall** — the Mineral realm; crystal caverns that store light like batteries. Ani's seat.
  **The Sifting** — the Dust realm; where everything ground small comes to rest, & where memory settles with it.
  **The Blank Grid** — a void of humming right angles, born when the Cogwold's gears first counted past infinity. Code is its weather.

## Beyond the Rim

Four places sit outside the Rimwheel & obey none of its directions.

**The Askew** — a realm of wrong angles where corners point inward. Its creatures are footnotes to geometry; it leaks wherever a seam is soldered badly.

**The Mistlock** — a closed domain of fog, where each prisoner rules a single grieving village & cannot leave. Its mists gather what the Pitchfrost loses.

**The Undercroft Registry** — a basement institution that catalogs & contains dangerous things in numbered cells. It is polite & very interested in Ani.

**The Starfold Prism** — a heavenly geometry of facets, each facet an oath given shape. Two of them matter: the **Rimevow Facet** (the Frozen Oath, "nothing shall sunder again") & the **Emberbond Facet** (the Burning Pact, "whatever is broken shall be forged anew"). The Rimeward was drawn with geometry borrowed from this prism.

## Hidden History

**The Bell Splitting** (the Third Sundering) is public history. The Rimwheel rang like a cracked bell, the Axlestone slipped from center, & the shores were renegotiated. In that treaty Geodehall was locked under Underheart: Mineral would ride beneath Earth, sheltered but sunless.

**The Quiet Shear** (the Fourth Sundering) is the secret. Before the Bell Splitting, a shadow city called **Duskmarrow** sat at the root fork beneath Antlerwild. When the lattice began to crack, its ruler cut one of the Morrowroot's taproots to keep the city from falling. The city fell anyway, into shadow, unhinged from every plane. The cut root became a wound in the lattice; the Bell Splitting was the bark tearing around it.

That ruler survives as **the Hollow Regent**, master of Duskmarrow. The Regent was also the first keeper of the Magpie Light, built to find the cut root again. Only the Sifting remembers this, because the sawdust of the Shear settled there. The Regent's lost tool lies there too: the **Needlesphere**, a compass orb of nested brass rings around a bead of root sap that swings toward the Morrowroot's wound whenever the lamp's beam passes through it.

## The One Story

The winter, the sliding wild, the ice ring, & the lighthouse are four faces of the same wound.

1. **The slide.** The cut taproot lies under Antlerwild's eastern reach. As the scar reopens, that edge loses its anchor & drifts toward the Churn. Herds vanish into fog. Antlerwild is where the tree must be replanted: a living cutting of the Morrowroot, set in the wound, is the only anchor strong enough to hold the realm in place.
2. **The Rimeward.** Generations ago the Ten Hearths raised a ring of ice across the dale's east: river water poured in layered courses, clockwork cold bartered from the Cogwold, & desperate geometry copied from the Starfold Prism. It holds the slide, but only by freezing everything near it.
3. **The Pitchfrost.** Ice drawn with the Rimevow's geometry carried the Frozen Oath with it. The oath woke as a living winter whose only aim is that nothing sunder again, even if it means nothing ever moves again.
4. **The Magpie Light.** A black & white tower on **Bearing Head**, the Rimeward's eastern headland, keeps the last true bearing: the direction of the cut taproot. Its lamp burns Leylit light. Each night lit costs the burning Leylit a year of memory, which is why it has gone dark.
5. **The seeds.** **Nightburrs** are dark seeds sown by the Hollow Regent. They root in leyline seams & solder planes shut. The Regent believes that if every plane is sealed around Duskmarrow, the city will be grafted back onto the root & the Shear undone. Each soldered seam pinches the sap, dims the Hearthsun, & swells the Gloamwound. The Pitchfrost welcomes them: sealed planes cannot sunder.

**Ani's place.** Geodehall's crystals store more light than any realm. Whoever leads it controls enough light to relight the Magpie Light without destroying a single Leylit, or enough to power a final soldering. That is why Ani's leadership is the prize. The Regent wants it to finish the seal, the Pitchfrost wants it frozen in place, & the Old Beast wants it kept underground.

**The Old Beast & the cost of the lock.** Underheart's ancient grazer of stone is no villain. It accepted Geodehall beneath itself to hide Mineral's light from the Regent. The price falls on Mineral: no sky, so its crystals slowly lose charge; Leylit born there are born dimmer; & Ani must pay a tithe of light every season to keep the Underheart's ceiling sealed. Ani resents the cage; the Beast resents being called a jailer. Their quarrel is the story's mid campaign hinge.

**The Sifting's role.** Every expedition into the Sifting turns up shards of the Shear: the Regent's oath, the cut, the first lamp. Dust is the realm that tells the truth, & the only place the bearing can be cross checked. It also holds the **Morrowseed**, a cutting that fell from the tree in the Quiet Shear & has waited there ever since.

**The Leylit.** Wisp lights that run the leylines like couriers along veins. Three elders lead them, called the **Kindled Moot**: the Ember Elder (memory), the Glint Elder (travel), & the Ash Elder (endings). They forbade anyone to light the Magpie lamp after the first keeper went missing.

## Cast

**Ani** — a Leylit of unusual brightness, a drifting will light whose glow shifts from quartz white to amber with mood. Ani holds the leadership of Geodehall: the Lantern Seat, the right to spend Mineral's stored light. Curious, stubborn, lonely.

**The Gravesworn** — an undead warrior from a barrow of old hunters, frost in its ribs, an ember of Ani's light in its chest. It travels with Ani as escort & conscience. It remembers dying to the Pitchfrost's first winter.

**The Hunter** — the player character: tall, rugged, short dark hair. Claims forty acres in the dale. He does not shapeshift; he has no bat form, ever.

**The Mother Aspect** — a young **glimmerhusk**, a dragon whose scales shine like moon pale feldspar. She is the protective, mothering face of the household, newly hatched into her strength, & she warms the hearth with her breath.

**The Snow Crowned Tracker** — white haired hunting partner; reads snow like script.

**The Hearth Father** — a seasoned hunter who stays at the homestead with the kids, runs the defenses, & never leaves during raids.

**The Fizzlet** — a crackling static sprite helper. It carries messages through frost, sparks fires, & blows up if overfed.

**The Eldest Child** tends the forge; **the Middle Child** keeps the stores; **the Youngest** talks to the Fizzlet & hears things in the ice no one else hears.

**The Churnglint** — a chaos eye avatar that takes the shape of an ice sprite. It appeared hovering over the **Tithe Chest** found under the homestead floor. The chest was forged by the first settler of the forty acres, who sealed it with blood to bind a shard of the Rimevow's oath to the land. That shard is why the homestead stays livable while the dale freezes. The blood stain is fresh because the seal is cracking; the Churnglint is the Churn looking through the crack. It trades riddles for warmth.

**The Hollow Regent** — master of Duskmarrow, first keeper of the Magpie Light, author of the Quiet Shear.

## The Pitchfrost

**Motive:** to keep its oath. It is the Frozen Oath given weather; it believes that motion is how worlds break, & it loves the dale the way ice loves a lake.

**Escalation clock** (advances one step at each season's end, or early when a Nightburr roots):

1. **Hoarstep** — frost at dawn; hunting yields drop by one.
2. **Glasswater** — rivers freeze; trade routes to the Ten Hearths close.
3. **Long Hush** — sound dies outdoors; possession becomes possible.
4. **Stillmarrow** — creatures & people freeze where they stand; raids double.
5. **Last Bell** — the dale stops. Game over.

**Possession:** from Long Hush on, a character who ends a turn outside warmth with 3 or more Chill is possessed for one round & acts under the winter's control. Possession breaks early if an ally spends a Warmth card or the Mother Aspect breathes on the target. A character possessed three times in one session becomes a frost thrall until cleansed at the Magpie Light.

**Gravesworn rule:** ally while its ember burns. The ember dims by one at each clock step & refills when Ani rests in Geodehall. At zero, or when possessed, it fights as a foe for that round. It can never be permanently lost: defeating it as a foe restores it as an ally with one ember.

**Defeat:** the Pitchfrost cannot be killed. It is answered. Relight the Magpie Light, plant the Morrowseed in Antlerwild, & present the Rimevow with the Emberbond's counter oath, & it becomes an ordinary winter.

## The Game

A **party game meets survival**, unlocked in layers:

1. **Board mode** — turn based hex board of the dale & the Rimwheel. Move, hunt, trade, roll events.
2. **Homestead** — build on the forty acres; assign the household; manage food, fuel, & warmth.
3. **Raids** — frost raiders, Nightburr thralls, & slide beasts assault the homestead in tactical rounds.
4. **Planar expeditions** — card driven trips through the Veilway into other realms.
5. **Card & item mode** — deckbuilding layer for abilities & artifacts.

**The Ten Hearths** — the frontier towns of **Rimmerdale**, the frozen northern reach of Antlerwild: Kettlecross (the market), Saltwick, Lowbraid, Tallowmoor, Fenspire, Drumrest, Haskeep, Owlgate, Brindle Ford, & Coldharrow (the town nearest the Rimeward).

**Players:** 1–4. Solo uses AI companions; online co op supports 2–4.

**Cooperative or competitive:** cooperative at heart. Everyone wins or loses together, but each player earns **Boasts** (feats, best hunt, boldest rescue) for party game bragging & cosmetic unlocks. Boasts never decide victory.

**Session end:** one session is one season, four turns on the board plus one raid. The session closes when the raid resolves; the clock then checks for advancement & the game autosaves. A full campaign runs four to eight seasons.

**Win:** relight the Magpie Light, deliver the true bearing to the cut taproot, & plant the Morrowseed in Antlerwild before Last Bell.

**Lose:** the clock reaches Last Bell; the homestead hearth goes cold for a full season; or Ani's light is extinguished.

**The kids & the homestead:** the children are never killed. In raids they can be frightened, which costs resources & morale, & the Hearth Father always stands with them. Their arcs grow with the seasons: the Eldest forges the lamp's new lens, the Middle Child's stores feed the final expedition, & the Youngest is the one who hears the Rimevow speak.

## Ending

The Sifting yields the last shard, the Hollow Regent's own oath sworn the night of the cut, & with it the Morrowseed. Ani faces the Kindled Moot & the Old Beast at once. The bargain: Ani lifts the lock & brings Geodehall up beside Underheart as an equal shore; in return, the Beast lends its weight to anchor Antlerwild while the root is mended.

At Bearing Head, the Eldest Child's lens is set. Ani lights the lamp from Geodehall's stored light, so no Leylit pays in memory. The beam passes through the Needlesphere, which swings east through the slide to the cut taproot beneath Duskmarrow. The Regent meets the party there, & the Hunter offers what the Regent sought for an age: the bearing. Together they plant the Morrowseed in the wound at the root fork, in the soil of Antlerwild, & it takes hold. With the root mended, the city needs no seal. The Nightburrs wither, sap rises, the Hearthsun steadies, & the Gloamwound shrinks to a cinder.

The Youngest speaks the Emberbond's counter oath to the Rimevow: *nothing shall sunder again, because what breaks shall be mended.* The Pitchfrost accepts & thaws into a hard but honest winter. The Rimeward melts back into the river. Duskmarrow rises as the seventeenth Hinge Town, its Regent its gatekeeper. The Tithe Chest closes clean; the Churnglint bows & returns to the Churn.

The new tree rises in Antlerwild's east, anchoring the realm against the Churn for good, & the hunt realm becomes the Rimwheel's second root. The forty acres become the **Eleventh Hearth**. The Gravesworn takes the Magpie Light's keeping, its ember now its own. Ani holds the Lantern Seat under open sky.

## Build Decisions

  **Engine:** Godot 4 with web export for browser play.
  **Order:** turn based board first; homestead, raids, expeditions, & cards layer on top.
  **V1 realms:** five interactive realms (Antlerwild with Rimmerdale as home, the Churn's eastern edge, Underheart, Geodehall, the Sifting). Every other realm appears as a board space & event card only.
  **Multiplayer:** online co op for 2–4, host authoritative.
  **Art:** a written style apocrypha first; all art generated from scratch to it. Palette: frost blues, ember ambers, magpie black & white.
  **Scope:** tight homestead focus for v1.
  **Mythic tie ins, expressed without borrowing:** a cosmos hung from one great tree, as in northern sagas; a long winter that comes before a world's end; a hunter mistress's untamed wild; lights in the marsh that lead travelers astray or home; a blood sealed hearth oath binding a household to its land; a guardian beast beneath the mountain. All names are new.

## V1 Numbers

**Starting resources (per player):** 6 Timber, 4 Stone, 3 Food, 2 Ember, 1 Lightshard. Household shares 10 Warmth.

**Hunt:** 1 Stamina + 1 Ember to enter snowfield; yield 1d4+1 Food (−1 per clock step). Big game: 2 Stamina, yield 2d4+2 Food plus 1 Hide.

**Build costs:**
| Structure | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Palisade segment | 3 Timber, 1 Stone | +2 raid defense |
| Smokehouse | 4 Timber, 2 Stone | Food doesn't spoil |
| Forge | 3 Stone, 2 Ember, 1 Lightshard | craft items |
| Warding Lantern | 2 Lightshard, 1 Stone | +3 Warmth radius; blocks possession |

**First raid:** 6 frost raiders, 3 HP & 1 damage each (total strength 18), arriving on the season's last turn.

**Starter cards:**
  **Hearth Spark** — 2 mana. Remove 2 Chill from one ally within 30 ft.
  **Antler Call** — 3 mana. Summon a hunt spirit for 2 rounds; 1d6 damage per round to one beast.
  **True Bearing** — 1 mana. Peek at the next event card & the clock's trigger.

**Item card: the Hushcube**
*Wondrous item, requires attunement.* A cube of grey crystal that holds a sliver of the Ebbing Dark.
  **Cost:** 7 mana. **Range:** 20 ft, one creature.
  **Effect:** target makes a Wisdom save. On failure it is stunned for 1 round & takes Xd8 necrotic damage, where X is the number of Nightburrs on the board (minimum 1).
  **Silence:** on failure, all Hope & Courage effects on the target end & cannot be applied until its next turn ends.
